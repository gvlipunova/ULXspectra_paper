\subsection{Fits with and without the transfer correction}
\label{sec:results:mcnomc}
% source: ULXspectra-mc docs/transfer_correction_results.md sect. 7, summaries/mc_vs_nomc_BH3a_BH4a.tsv (b551655);
%         chi2 of the saved best fit, sigma = 0.08 dex (PLAN C24)

To see what the transfer correction (App.~\ref{app:mc}) does to the fits, we fitted all 14 sources with BH3a and BH4a over 0.3--10\,keV with and without it.
With the correction the fit is better or equal in 26 of the 28 pairs; it is slightly worse only for BH3a of M101ULS ($\chi^2 = 153$ against 149) and NGC55 (1.91 against 1.21).
The largest gains are for BH4a: M101ULS ($\chi^2$ from 1438 to 3.95), NGC247SS (1897 to 734), M51ULS (655 to 412), NGC1313H (74.8 to 19.4) and M81ULS (53.1 to 0.47); for BH3a, HolmIX (35.5 to 10.0) and NGC247SS (452 to 355).

\textbf{Without the correction} the model \textbf{funnel is too faint}, by a factor of 1.2--3.5 (App.~\ref{app:mc:bestfits}), and the fits move in inclination to compensate.
The BH3a fits go towards face-on (M51ULS from $49^\circ$ to $4^\circ$, NGC247SS from $47^\circ$ to $2^\circ$, NGC6946 from $47^\circ$ to $5^\circ$, NGC300 from $56^\circ$ to $22^\circ$), and the BH4a fits mostly towards edge-on (NGC1313H from $1^\circ$ to $68^\circ$, NGC5408 from $35^\circ$ to $79^\circ$, NGC1313B from $22^\circ$ to $68^\circ$, NGC247SS from $62^\circ$ to $86^\circ$).
The physical parameters change too.
For HolmIX, BH3a with the correction is a supercritical wind solution ($\dot m_0 = 2.6$, $\epsilon_{\rm w} = 0.02$), while without it the fit is subcritical ($\dot m_0 = 0.84$) and leaves the wind parameters unconstrained (Fig.~\ref{fig:holmix_mcnomc} in App.~\ref{app:mc:nomc}).
Other shifts by $3\sigma$ or more: M51ULS BH3a, $\dot m_0 = 672$ against 30 and $\epsilon_{\rm w} = 0.02$ against 0.92; M101ULS BH4a, $M = 2900$ against $106\,M_\odot$ and $\rin = 1.1$ against $18\,\rg$; NGC1313H BH4a, $\dot m_0 = 21$ against 0.94; NGC247SS BH4a, $\dot m_0 = 284$ against 33.
